Who we are

Three decades of chemistry, built in Melbourne.

Founded in 1992, Marchem has grown from a chemical distributor into one of the region's leading specialists in automotive and industrial chemical technology.

From our Brooklyn, Victoria headquarters we formulate, test, blend, package and deliver. That gives our customers one accountable partner from raw material sourcing to the finished product on the shelf.

  • In-house laboratory and automotive R&D centre in Melbourne
  • Strategic partnerships with leading global chemical manufacturers
  • Operations in Australia, New Zealand, Thailand, China and Indonesia
