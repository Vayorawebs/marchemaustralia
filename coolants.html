Why it matters

Cooling systems are more complex than ever.

Modern engines, battery packs, power electronics and industrial equipment run hotter, tighter and longer than ever before. The fluid that protects them has to keep up.

Marchem develops coolant solutions for internal combustion, hybrid, electric and fuel-cell vehicles, as well as industrial, data-centre and emerging applications. Each formulation is tested in our Melbourne laboratory to ASTM methods.

We work closely with leading OEM factory-fill and OES suppliers, and hold the most OEM approvals in our region.

Applications

One partner for every thermal challenge.

Whatever the powertrain or process, we can match or develop the right fluid.

Internal combustion

Long-life engine coolants that protect against corrosion, overheating and freezing in passenger, commercial and heavy-duty vehicles.

Hybrid vehicles

Coolants for dual-system hybrid architectures that manage both engine and electric drivetrain temperatures.

Electric vehicles

Thermal-management fluids for battery packs, motors and power electronics in battery-electric vehicles.

Fuel-cell vehicles

Solutions for the demanding thermal requirements of hydrogen fuel-cell systems.

Industrial heat transfer

Heat-transfer fluids for industrial plant, stationary engines and process cooling.

Data-centre cooling

Fluids for the liquid-cooling systems that keep critical digital infrastructure running.
