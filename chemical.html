A legacy of excellence

From distributor to diversified group.

Marchem began as a chemical distributor and has grown into a diversified group serving automotive, industrial, transport, manufacturing, mining and agricultural customers.

Today we are trusted by global brands, OEMs and distributors, with offices and partners in Thailand, China and Indonesia supporting our Australian operations.

  • Strategic partnerships with leading global chemical manufacturers
  • More than 30 million litres of chemicals imported
  • Raw-material sourcing through to warehousing and delivery
