Our origin

Proudly Australian. Regionally connected.

For more than three decades Marchem has built a reputation for innovation, reliability and supply-chain strength across Australia and Southeast Asia.

Our story

From distributor to an integrated chemical group.

Founded in 1992, Marchem is now one of the region's leading specialists in automotive and industrial chemical technology. We distribute, manufacture and develop automotive fluids and industrial chemicals for OEM, aftermarket, transport, mining, industrial and commercial customers.

Our range spans coolants, brake fluids, AdBlue®, degreasers, cleaning chemicals and specialty fluids, all made to international standards. Strategic global partnerships let us supply leading chemical and automotive brands throughout the region.

With offices and operations in Australia, New Zealand, China, Thailand and Indonesia, we combine efficient logistics and local market knowledge with global sourcing.

Our laboratory and R&D facilities sit at the heart of the business, equipped for modern testing, quality control and product validation. Today the group continues to expand into transport, packaging and integrated supply-chain services, offering a complete service from raw-material sourcing to manufacturing, packaging, warehousing and distribution.
